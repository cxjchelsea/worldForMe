# 02 来源物象与来源谱系

## Orphic / Greek world egg

Orphic cosmogonic materials provide a strong source family in which a primordial/world egg participates directly in cosmic generation and the birth of Phanes/Protogonos.

## Indian Brahmāṇḍa / Hiraṇyagarbha family

印度宇宙论中 egg / golden germ / brahmāṇḍa 形成长期而多层的宇宙生成模型；需区分 Vedic Hiraṇyagarbha、后续 cosmic egg 解释与 Purāṇic cosmology，不把不同年代材料压成单一文本。
