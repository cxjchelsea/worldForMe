# Slavic / Eastern European Earth-diver

## Evidence summary

Scholarly discussion of Slavic creation narratives records Earth-diver variants in which a primeval ocean precedes land, a devil / bird or other actor dives to the bottom, retrieves earth, and the recovered material participates in land or world formation.

```text

R2 underwater_descent_for_earth_material = PASS
R3 retrieved_earth_material = PASS
R4 retrieved_material_causes_land_formation = PASS
```
