# 04 跨传统分布

Earth-diver 在比较神话与民俗学中具有广泛分布，但“分布广”不等于“单一起源”。

## High structural match

### Haudenosaunee / Iroquois

```text
water-dominant lower world
+ underwater retrieval
+ earth material
+ land formation
= FULL MATCH
```

### Slavic / Eastern European variants

```text
primeval ocean
+ diver descends
+ earth retrieved
+ land/world formation
= FULL MATCH
```

## Wider comparative distribution

比较研究还记录 North American、Siberian、South / Southeast Asian 等区域的 Earth-diver family。

这些分布信息用于说明：

```text
motif family is geographically broad
```

但没有足够证据时，不写成：

```text
historical transmission supported
```

## 分布分类

```text
Haudenosaunee / Iroquois
→ high structural match

Slavic / Eastern European
→ high structural match

wider Siberian / Asian / North American reports
→ distribution support; individual source verification pending

post-flood Earth-diver stories
→ structural variant; primary-domain caution
```

## 治理结论

```text
cross_tradition_distribution
≠ transmission_history

same retrieval mechanism
≠ common historical origin
```
