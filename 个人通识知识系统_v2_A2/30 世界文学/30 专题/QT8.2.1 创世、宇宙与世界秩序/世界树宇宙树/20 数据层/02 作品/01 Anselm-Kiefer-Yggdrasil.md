# Anselm Kiefer｜Yggdrasil

The Metropolitan Museum of Art explicitly identifies Kiefer's work with the Old Norse Yggdrasil and explains the artist's self-identification with the tree, connecting the work to transformation, growth and decay.

This is therefore a documented `symbol_reuse`, not a merely tree-like visual analogy.
