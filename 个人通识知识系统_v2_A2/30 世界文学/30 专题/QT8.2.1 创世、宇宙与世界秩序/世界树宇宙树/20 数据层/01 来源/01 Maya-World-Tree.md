# Maya World Tree

## Evidence summary

Maya cosmic-tree evidence supports:

```text
vertical_cosmic_connection
cosmic_center_or_axis
ordered_multi_realm_world
```

Smithsonian NMAI describes the ceiba as axis mundi / world tree connecting Earth and sky; Met material further identifies the great central tree with roots in the Underworld and branches in the heavens and situates it at the cosmic center.
