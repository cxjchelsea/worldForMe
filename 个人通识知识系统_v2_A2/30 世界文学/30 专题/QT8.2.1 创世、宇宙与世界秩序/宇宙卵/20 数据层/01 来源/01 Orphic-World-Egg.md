# Orphic World Egg

Orphic cosmogonic materials provide a strong source family in which a primordial/world egg is directly tied to cosmic generation and the emergence of Phanes/Protogonos.

This record supports the symbol's early association with primordial totality, generative potential and cosmic birth. It does not claim that every later cosmic-egg tradition descends historically from Orphic materials.
