# Indian Brahmāṇḍa / Cosmic Egg

Indian cosmological traditions provide a long-lived egg/cosmic-container model. This record intentionally keeps Hiraṇyagarbha, Brahmāṇḍa and later Purāṇic formulations historically distinct rather than collapsing them into one source text.

It supports stable meanings of cosmic totality, generative potential and gestation of the cosmos.
