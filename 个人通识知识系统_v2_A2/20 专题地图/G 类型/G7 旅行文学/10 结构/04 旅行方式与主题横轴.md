# 旅行方式与主题横轴

原专题20条横轴大致可以分为四组：

**旅行目的**
- Pilgrimage
- Knowledge
- Self-Cultivation
- Homecoming

**观看与权力**
- Otherness
- Travel Gaze
- Empire
- Women Travellers
- Postcolonial Reverse Gaze

**空间与交通**
- Nature / Wilderness
- City
- Road
- Railway
- Walking
- River
- Polar / Desert

**时间与身份**
- Tourist vs Traveller
- Travel + Memory
- Travel + War
- Scientific Travel

这些关系写入 `travel_axes`，允许一本作品同时处于多个旅行坐标。
